## What the site is for

Sonoran Swap is a concept Phoenix desert-landscaping company that turns grass lawns into water-wise desert yards. The site lets a homeowner outline their lawn on a satellite map, see the city rebate they could get (Phoenix, Mesa and Chandler rules from the cities' own pages; Scottsdale's status), preview their own yard as a desert garden, check eligibility before removing anything, and book a free yard walk. It is a concept business: bookings are demonstrations and nothing on the site claims customers, reviews or results it doesn't have.
